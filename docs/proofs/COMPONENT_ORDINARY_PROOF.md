# The ordinary flip-component HRV formula

Status: complete mathematical proof, independently audited by primary, Astra
and Terra. The finite strict/e component identity is now Lean-verified; see
[the completion audit](../COMPONENT_COMPLETION.md). The proof applies to every
ordinary natural graph with any subset of its corners strict. It requires
neither saturation, connectedness, a forest hypothesis nor component-isolation
assumptions. The circular case is supplied by the separate
[Overleaf v9 companion](COMPONENT_CIRCULAR_FACE_PROOF.md).

## Statement and previously established semantic facts

Fix an ordinary interval-closed natural graph G on the linearly ordered
vertices 1,...,n, a set S of strict corners, and a COMPLETE flip component C.
A state is a selected forward relation A=N∪S. The two exchanged shorts of
a flip are normal and its retained long and selected short are reachability
covers. Write K(A) for its strict-coloring enumerator on any fixed finite
alphabet X, and E(A)=e_(HRV fiber sizes of A)(X). We prove

    Σ_(A∈C) K(A) = Σ_(A∈C) E(A).

The right side is an elementary-positive expression. All component states
have the same normal ascent number, so the common shifted q-weight may be
restored. The strict/e version corresponds to the draft's weak/h version
under the same omega convention used in the existing component interface.

We use the proved [labeled-poset refinement](COMPONENT_MIXED_POSET.md).
For a permutation π let Z_π be the total-order enumerator, strict at numeric
ascents of π and weak at descents. Let j_π be the evaluated signed HRV
valuation of that total mixed order. If m_C(π) counts the states of C whose
selected relations have π as a linear extension, that refinement gives

    K(C)=Σ_π m_C(π) Z_π,       E(C)=Σ_π m_C(π) j_π.

The same note proves that j_π is the signed sum of elementary monomials over
partitions of π into contiguous mountain blocks: each block increases then
decreases, its maximum is larger than every later block maximum, and its
sign is (−1)^(the number of its internal descents). These facts were proved
from the exact HRV-map coefficient formula, without the component theorem.

## A four-word identity inside one complete component

For a<b<c and arbitrary fixed exterior words ρ,σ containing the other
vertices, we have

    m_C(ρ acb σ)+m_C(ρ bca σ)
      =m_C(ρ cab σ)+m_C(ρ bac σ).                 (1)

Swapping the adjacent entries a,c in acb versus cab changes compatibility
only for the possible selected edge ac. Ordinary selected edges all point
forward, so their difference counts exactly the ac-selected states compatible
with acb. Similarly the difference bac minus bca counts the ac-selected
states compatible with bac. If ac is not natural, both differences vanish.

Suppose ac is natural. Its shorts ab,bc are natural by interval closure,
and neither can be strict: each is a proper subinterval of a natural edge,
whereas strict edges are maximal natural intervals. In acb, bc must be absent;
in bac, ab must be absent. The states with BOTH shorts absent are the same
on both sides. The remaining states are paired by

    (A, ρ acb σ) ↔ (A-ab+bc, ρ bac σ),

retaining ac. This is an ACTUAL flip. In the source, a,c are consecutive
positions, so ac is a cover. The only possible intermediate vertex in a
path from a to b would be c, which cannot point backward to b. Thus ab is
a cover too. The inverse cover tests follow by the same argument with the
target word; alternatively use the equivalent source-cover criterion.
All exterior vertices stay before or after the entire triple, so every
unchanged selected relation remains compatible. Completeness of C keeps
both paired states in THIS component. This proves (1).

This is a bijection of orientation/permutation occurrences, not a claim
that a single flip preserves repeated-color assignments.

## The finite word algebra

Let R be the free associative integer algebra on u_1,...,u_n, modulo the
two-sided ideal generated by

- every word with a repeated letter;
- u_a u_c u_b+u_b u_c u_a−u_c u_a u_b−u_b u_a u_c, for a<b<c.

All equations below are in R, with central scalar variables adjoined.
Words of length greater than n vanish. In particular every 1+t u_i is
invertible with inverse 1−t u_i. More generally

    (1+t u_i) Y (1−t u_i)=Y+t[u_i,Y],

since u_i Y u_i vanishes for every Y. We write Ad_D(Y)=DYD^(-1).

Extend m_C to words by zero unless they are full permutations of the vertex
set. It annihilates the ideal: repeated-letter words remain repeated in any
context; a contextual four-word relation either has wrong content, giving
four zeros, or is exactly (1). Thus m_C defines an integer-linear functional
on R. It also extends linearly over the scalar polynomial ring.

This is where the proof keeps the original component label. It never combines
different unknown components to invoke an aggregate formula.

## Two elementary conjugation identities

For an initial set of lower labels put

    A=Σ_(i<n) u_i,       D(t)=∏_(i=n−1,...,1) (1+t u_i).

First,

    Ad_D(t)(u_n)=u_n+t[A,u_n].                    (2)

Indeed, conjugation is the ordered product of operators 1+t ad_(u_i).
Any term using two or more of them has two innermost indices j<i<n and
contains [u_i,[u_j,u_n]], which is zero by the four-word relation. Only
the constant and single-operator terms survive.

Second, for every initial alphabet,

    [Σ_(i≤n)u_i, ∏_(i=n,...,1)(1+t u_i)]=0.      (3)

We prove this by induction. Assume [A,D]=0 for the lower alphabet. Equation
(2) gives [u_n,D]=−t[A,u_n]D. Therefore

    [A+u_n,(1+t u_n)D]
      =t[A,u_n]D+(1+t u_n)[u_n,D]
      =−t² u_n[A,u_n]D=0.

The last expression has u_n repeated. The empty alphabet starts the
induction. No external commutation theorem is used.

Set T=ad_A. From (2), (3), and D commuting with A, we obtain

    Ad_D(t)(T^k u_n)=T^k u_n+t T^(k+1)u_n.

These iterates vanish for k≥n by repeated-letter vanishing. Inverting the
operator on their span yields

    Ad_D(t)^(-1)(u_n)=Σ_(k≥0)(−t)^k T^k u_n.

On the other hand D(t)^(-1)=∏_(i=1,...,n−1)(1−t u_i). Expanding its
conjugation and comparing coefficients gives

    T^k u_n=Σ_(i_1<...<i_k<n)
                 ad_(u_i1) ... ad_(u_ik)(u_n).  (4)

There is no factorial. Expanding each nested commutator gives a signed sum
of mountain words: a subset of the chosen labels increases before u_n, the
remaining labels decrease after it, and the sign counts the latter labels.

## Factoring the finite-alphabet coloring product

For X=(x_1,...,x_m) define

    D_n(t)=∏_(i=n,...,1)(1+t u_i),
    F_n(X)=D_n(x_1)...D_n(x_m),       F_0(X)=1.

Write D=D_(n−1) and A=Σ_(i<n)u_i. Since D_n(x_t)=(1+x_t u_n)D(x_t),
and every term using u_n twice vanishes, right multiplication by
F_(n−1)(X)^(-1) gives

    F_n F_(n−1)^(-1)
      =1+Σ_(t=1)^m x_t Ad_(D(x_1)...D(x_(t−1)))(u_n).

This cancellation preserves the order of all D factors; it does not assume
that they commute with one another. On the span of T^k u_n each prefix
conjugation acts as ∏_(s<t)(1+x_s T). Consequently

    F_n F_(n−1)^(-1)
      =1+Σ_(k≥0) e_(k+1)(X) T^k u_n.            (5)

Here we used Σ_t x_t e_k(x_1,...,x_(t−1))=e_(k+1)(X).
By (4), write the nonconstant part of (5) as W_n(X), a signed sum of mountain
words with peak n and coefficient e_(word length)(X). Iterating (5) gives

    F_n(X)=(1+W_n(X))...(1+W_1(X)).              (6)

These identities hold for every finite m, including m=0, and use only finite
sums and products. No symmetric-functions completion or infinite series is
needed.

## Pairing proves the full component formula

Take the explicit free-word representative of the left side of (6). Each
color contributes a decreasing block of vertices. Thus the coefficient of
a full permutation π is exactly Z_π: equal colors occur along descending
runs, while an ascent forces a strict increase. Applying m_C gives K(C).

For the explicit expanded right side of (6), full-permutation words consist
of disjoint mountain blocks with decreasing maxima. Their coefficients are
exactly j_π by the previously proved total-order HRV formula. Applying m_C
therefore gives E(C).

Equation (6) is an equality IN THE QUOTIENT. It is not a coefficientwise
assertion that Z_π=j_π, which is generally false. The functional m_C kills
the ideal, so pairing the two representatives is legitimate and proves

    K(C)=E(C).

All uses of the component hypothesis occurred in the actual-flip pairing
for (1). This supplies the required component isolation directly. In
particular the proof does not assume any unresolved DN packet isolation,
active uniqueness, closure-fiber connectivity, or smaller-component
multiplicity assertion from the earlier induction approaches.

## Scope, verification and formalization boundary

The proof covers arbitrary ordinary corner decorations and all components,
including disconnected states, isolated vertices and the empty diagram.
Together with the proved [compatible-lift transport](COMPONENT_AMBIENT_REDUCTION.md),
it gives the formula for balanced circular components in prose as well.
The word algebra above uses one finite linear order. The
[Overleaf v9 bounded-face companion](COMPONENT_CIRCULAR_FACE_PROOF.md) now
handles all unbalanced circular components by reduction to balanced ones,
without a periodic version of this algebra. That draft also gives a direct
nested-commutator HRV extraction which shortens the semantic refinement.

Primary, Astra and Terra independently checked the component pairing,
conjugation signs, inverse coefficient comparison, order of the factors,
mountain coefficient interpretation and use of the quotient functional.
Luna separately audited the Lean interfaces. No enumeration or compilation
was used to substitute for any uniform proof step.
