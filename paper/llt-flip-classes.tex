Flip classes and elementary expansions\\

\begin{abstract}
After the substitution $q\mapsto q+1$, vertical-strip LLT polynomials are
weighted sums of strict $P$-partition generating functions indexed by
orientations of decorated unit-interval graphs, with weight
$q^{\asc(\theta)}$.
A flip moves an ascending edge across a triangle while keeping its long edge.
We show that the sum over every flip class is elementary-positive: it equals the
sum of the elementary symmetric functions given by the highest reachable
vertices of its orientations.
This answers Hwang's question on the Schur positivity of LLT flip-class sums,
and it holds for circular unit-interval graphs as well.
Summing over classes gives a new proof of the elementary expansion of
Alexandersson and Sulzgruber, and its circular analog, which was open.
We also obtain a flip-class refinement of the elementary expansion of
LLT cumulants.
\end{abstract}
